

## CARDHOLDER'S DECLARATIONS, RESERVATION OF RIGHTS AND NON-WAIVER

### 1. No admission

The Cardholder makes this declaration solely for the purpose of establishing and clarifying the factual record concerning the formal dispute / chargeback process.

Nothing in this declaration shall be construed as an admission by the Cardholder of any fact, allegation, conclusion, liability, validity of the underlying transaction, adequacy of the merchant's performance, or correctness of any decision communicated by Axis Bank, unless expressly stated by the Cardholder.

Where the Cardholder records a fact based upon information or documents supplied by Axis Bank or another party, that fact is identified as such and shall not be treated as an independent admission by the Cardholder.

### 2. No waiver of rights or remedies

The Cardholder expressly reserves all rights, remedies, claims, objections and legal or regulatory recourse available under applicable law, regulation, contract, card-network rules, consumer-protection law and other applicable mechanisms.

Participation in this declaration process, supplying information, answering questions, signing this declaration, or requesting clarification from Axis Bank shall not constitute a waiver, release, settlement, compromise, discharge or abandonment of any such right, remedy, claim or objection.

### 3. No settlement or acceptance of Axis Bank's position

This declaration shall not be interpreted as acceptance of any previous statement, explanation, chargeback determination, merchant-bank response, internal conclusion or other position communicated by Axis Bank.

In particular, requesting Axis Bank to identify and certify the factual history of the formal chargeback process shall not constitute acceptance that the process was correctly conducted or that the resulting decision was correct.

### 4. No restriction on subsequent evidence

The Cardholder reserves the right to submit additional documents, correspondence, photographs, records, communications or other evidence that subsequently becomes available or is identified as relevant.

The absence of a document from this declaration shall not constitute an admission that such document does not exist, was not previously supplied, or is not relevant.

### 5. Accuracy of the Cardholder's statements

The Cardholder has provided the information in this declaration based on the documents and information presently available to the Cardholder.

Where a matter cannot be established from the Cardholder's records, the Cardholder has not represented that matter as an established fact.

Similarly, where the answer depends exclusively upon records maintained by Axis Bank, the merchant, the acquiring/merchant bank, or the card network, the Cardholder does not make any representation as to the contents of those records.

### 6. Distinction between fact and conclusion

The Cardholder requests that factual events be distinguished from conclusions or interpretations.

In particular, the following should be separately identified where applicable:

(a) what document or communication was sent;

(b) when it was sent;

(c) to whom it was sent;

(d) what evidence was attached or transmitted;

(e) what response was received;

(f) what decision was subsequently made; and

(g) the rule, policy or contractual provision relied upon for that decision.

A statement that a transaction was "valid", that "service has been rendered", or that a chargeback was "rejected" shall not by itself be treated as establishing the factual circumstances giving rise to that conclusion.

### 7. Formal process and subsequent good-faith communication

The Cardholder expressly distinguishes the formal dispute / chargeback process from any subsequent communication or request made by Axis Bank on a "good faith", goodwill or other non-formal basis after the formal chargeback process was stated by Axis Bank to have been exhausted.

Unless expressly identified otherwise, the interactions recorded in this declaration concern the formal dispute / chargeback process only.

The Cardholder does not intend that a subsequent good-faith request be treated retrospectively as proof that a particular document was submitted, considered or adjudicated during the earlier formal chargeback process.

### 8. Requests concerning Axis Bank's own records

Where a question in this declaration concerns an action performed by Axis Bank or a communication sent or received by Axis Bank, the Cardholder requests that Axis Bank answer the question based upon its records.

If the requested fact cannot be established from the records available to Axis Bank, the Cardholder requests that the answer expressly state:

> "Not ascertainable from the records available to us."

The Cardholder does not request Axis Bank to create or reconstruct a record that does not exist.

### 9. Non-disclosure does not convert into a factual answer

If Axis Bank is unable or unwilling to disclose a communication, document, identity or other information because of a legal, regulatory, contractual, card-network, confidentiality or other restriction, the Cardholder requests that Axis Bank identify the applicable basis for such non-disclosure to the extent legally permissible.

Where disclosure of the underlying document is prohibited but the underlying factual question can nevertheless be answered, the Cardholder requests a factual answer without requiring disclosure of protected material.

### 10. No authorization to make adverse use of ambiguity

The Cardholder does not consent to an ambiguous, incomplete or qualified response being represented as a definitive factual admission by the Cardholder.

Where a question cannot be answered definitively, the Cardholder requests that Axis Bank identify the answer as "YES", "NO", "OTHER", or "NOT ASCERTAINABLE FROM RECORDS", as applicable, together with a concise explanation.

### 11. Preservation of records

The Cardholder requests that Axis Bank preserve, in accordance with its applicable legal and regulatory record-retention obligations, the records relevant to the formal dispute / chargeback process, including records concerning communications, documents submitted, responses received and decisions taken.

This request is made without asserting any particular retention period beyond what is required or applicable to Axis Bank.

### 12. Regulatory and legal basis for this declaration

This declaration is intended to facilitate a clear factual record and is not intended to create a contractual obligation by unilateral declaration.

The Cardholder relies, among other things, upon the regulatory framework governing credit-card issuers and grievance redressal.

The RBI Master Direction – Credit Card and Debit Card – Issuance and Conduct Directions, 2022 requires card issuers to maintain internal records enabling operations to be traced and errors to be rectified, and requires a grievance-redress mechanism within the card-issuing entity. It also provides that the designated grievance-redress officer should ensure that cardholder grievances are redressed promptly.

The RBI Integrated Ombudsman framework provides for the Regulated Entity to submit its written response and documents relied upon in relation to a complaint and provides for relevant material submitted by one party to be furnished to the other party, subject to the applicable framework.

Accordingly, the purpose of this declaration is to obtain a clear factual record of the Bank's own handling of the formal dispute rather than to require disclosure of information that the Bank is legally prohibited from disclosing.

### 13. No prejudice

Nothing in this declaration shall prejudice the Cardholder's position in any existing or future proceeding, complaint, representation, consumer proceeding, arbitration, civil proceeding, regulatory proceeding, or other lawful remedy.

The Cardholder expressly reserves the right to rely upon this declaration, the Bank's responses, the underlying documents, and subsequently obtained evidence in any forum having jurisdiction over the matter.

### 14. Reliance on Bank's certification

Where an authorised officer of Axis Bank signs a certification concerning the Bank's records, the Cardholder will understand that certification as a representation by the Bank concerning the records available to the Bank and the factual matters expressly certified therein.

Nothing in the Cardholder's signature shall convert the Bank's certification into a statement made or adopted by the Cardholder.

### 15. Limited purpose of signature

The Cardholder's signature, if placed on any copy of this declaration, shall signify only that the Cardholder has submitted the document and identified the factual questions and reservations contained in it.

It shall not signify acceptance of any response subsequently inserted by Axis Bank, acceptance of any undisclosed document, waiver of any right, or settlement of the underlying dispute unless a separate written settlement is expressly executed for that purpose.
